## Confirmed product principles

1. Viewer experience is the central value proposition.
2. The economic case must translate the experience benefit into defensible revenue or margin impact.
3. The complete feature suite remains the long-term vision.
4. The first viable release will be a smaller pilot designed to create an observable customer win.
5. Claims will be labelled as assumptions, hypotheses, targets, benchmarks, or validated findings.

## Phase 2 outcome

Independent secondary research supports bedtime viewing as a credible context and manual timers as an established pattern. It does not validate the incidence of falling asleep during paid streaming, recovery-card value, unattended playback, retention, Premium conversion, or Netflix-specific savings.

Gate 2 was approved on 2026-11-08 as a conditional pass into a narrowed manual timer, checkpoint, and recovery pilot. Wearable triggers, advanced audio, therapeutic claims, fixed global activation windows, and quantified financial outcomes are excluded.

## Phase 5 backlog

The controlling backlog is [DR-39 — Netflix After Hours: Phase 5 Future Evidence](https://dumsworkspace-37513198.atlassian.net/browse/DR-39). It contains 12 linked stories and preserves the following boundaries:

- no participant outreach without research governance and recruitment authorization;
- no Netflix affiliation or sponsorship implication;
- no retention, conversion, savings, or ROI claim without measured and authorized evidence;
- wearable and cross-device capabilities remain separate feasibility tracks; and
- Gate 5 must decide among stop, revise/research, continue learning pilot, or seek implementation approval.

## Evidence integrity

All metrics currently present in the concept materials must be treated as projected targets until supported by research or experimentation. References to completed UAT, production-service readiness, measured savings, or measured conversion are superseded by this status statement. Static prototype deployment demonstrates the interaction design only.
