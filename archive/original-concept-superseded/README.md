# Original Concept — Superseded

**Superseded on:** 2026-11-09  
**Replacement:** Evidence-aligned pilot concept documented in Phases 1–4 and the root interactive prototype

This archive preserves the original pre-research product, technical, delivery, strategy, and prototype artifacts. They are retained for decision traceability and portfolio learning, not as the current recommendation.

## Why the concept changed

Independent secondary research supported late-night viewing as a credible context and manual timers as an established interaction pattern. It did not validate Netflix-specific incidence, retention, Premium conversion, bandwidth savings, health outcomes, or wearable-trigger performance. The active release therefore narrows the first viable concept to:

- manual 15-, 30-, and 45-minute timers;
- an end-of-episode choice;
- a cancellable dim-and-stop transition;
- a locally saved checkpoint; and
- two explicit resume choices.

Wearables, therapeutic or sleep-quality claims, binaural audio, Premium prompts, and quantified commercial outcomes are excluded from the active pilot.
