All language below describes independent portfolio work. The case was not produced for, endorsed by, or affiliated with Netflix. Customer outcomes and financial results remain unmeasured.

## LinkedIn announcement

I have completed Netflix After Hours, an independent product management case exploring a simple question: how might a streaming service create a better late-night viewing experience for people who fall asleep while watching?

The work began as a broad feature suite. Evidence changed the product. I narrowed the pilot to viewer-controlled timers, a cancellable stop, a locally saved checkpoint, and an explicit resume choice. I then built and verified a deployment-ready interactive prototype, defined the measurement model, and tested the economic logic.

The final conclusion matters as much as the prototype: the viewer context is credible, but customer desirability and a positive financial case have not been demonstrated. I kept retention and premium contribution at zero rather than presenting hypotheses as results.

The case shows how I approach AI and digital products: frame the problem, challenge assumptions, reduce scope when evidence requires it, build enough to learn, and make the decision traceable.

## 60-second interview explanation

Netflix After Hours is an independent portfolio case, not work completed inside Netflix. I explored a late-night streaming problem: viewers can fall asleep, wake several episodes ahead, and lose their true stopping point. I started with a broad concept, then created a gated evidence process covering research, experience design, feasibility, measurement, and economics. Secondary research supported the viewing context and timer pattern, but it did not validate frequency, desirability, retention, conversion, or savings. I therefore narrowed the pilot to manual timers, a cancellable dim-and-stop sequence, a local checkpoint, and an explicit resume choice. I built and verified a public interactive prototype and a formula-driven value model. My final recommendation was disciplined: the pilot is bounded and deployable, but the positive financial case is not demonstrated without authorized customer research and internal cost data. The case shows product judgment, not just feature design.
