# Gate 4 Decision Recommendation

**Recommendation date:** 2026-11-08  
**Approval date:** 2026-11-09  
**Gate question:** Is there a positive value case under conservative assumptions?  
**Approved disposition:** Gate 4 review complete; positive financial case not demonstrated

## Decision basis

| Requirement | Status | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |

| Operational quantity computable | Met conditionally | Requires labelled adoption, completion, and avoided-minute assumptions |
| Retention effect supported | Not met | No causal customer evidence |
| Premium conversion supported | Not met | No observed conversion evidence |
| Marginal delivery value available | Not met | Internal architecture economics unavailable |
| Implementation and certification cost available | Not met | Requires authorized engineering estimate |
| Annual operating cost available | Not met | Requires authorized operating estimate |
| Positive conservative net contribution | Not demonstrated | Material inputs are missing |

## Recommendation

Do not use the scenario outputs to claim revenue, savings, ROI, or a positive Netflix investment case.

For portfolio purposes, the defensible conclusion is:

> Netflix After Hours has a plausible viewer and operational value mechanism. Its financial attractiveness remains unproven because the required behavioural, marginal-cost, and implementation inputs are not publicly available.

## Gate disposition

Gate 4 is closed as **not demonstrated**, rather than failed. The product hypothesis is not disproven; the investment conclusion cannot be evaluated with the evidence available to an independent portfolio case. Approval of this disposition is not approval of a positive business case.

## Conditions for reopening Gate 4

1. Authorized telemetry establishes eligible-session volume and avoided continuation minutes.
2. An experiment estimates activation, controlled-stop completion, correction, and unwanted-interruption rates.
3. Internal finance or infrastructure owners supply marginal delivery value.
4. Engineering supplies implementation, certification, and ongoing operating estimates.
5. The conservative case remains positive with retention and Premium contribution held at zero.

## Portfolio maturity recommendation

After documenting this decision, the case may be presented as a completed concept-to-decision portfolio case with an evidence-bounded investment conclusion. It must not be presented as UAT-complete, production-ready, customer-validated, or financially approved.
