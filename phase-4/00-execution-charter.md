# Phase 4 Value-Case Charter

**Status:** Complete  
**Authorization:** Gate 3 conditionally approved on 2026-11-08  
**Project posture:** Independent portfolio case; no internal Netflix economics or customer telemetry

## Objective

Determine whether the evidence available to this portfolio case supports a positive value conclusion under conservative assumptions, while preserving a usable model for authorized internal inputs.

## Value hierarchy

1. **Viewer value:** intentional stopping and transparent recovery;
2. **Operational quantity:** avoided continuation minutes and stream-hours;
3. **Financial translation:** marginal delivery value, retention contribution, or Premium contribution;
4. **Net contribution:** total quantified benefit less implementation and operating cost.

## Rules

- Phase 3 synthetic metrics are computation examples, not forecast inputs.
- Netflix audience volume, unattended-playback incidence, marginal delivery economics, engineering cost, retention effect, and conversion effect are unavailable.
- The model uses 100,000 eligible sessions per month as a normalization unit, not a Netflix volume estimate.
- Low, Base, and High physical scenarios are illustrative assumptions, not forecasts.
- Retention and Premium contribution remain zero in the conservative treatment.
- Delivery contribution remains zero until a defensible marginal value per avoided stream-hour is supplied.
- A positive Gate 4 conclusion requires implementation and operating costs.

## Deliverables

1. Formula-driven value model;
2. normalized physical scenario comparison;
3. break-even structure;
4. evidence and assumption register;
5. Gate 4 decision recommendation.

## Decision standard

Gate 4 passes only when the conservative case includes complete benefit and cost inputs and produces positive net contribution without depending on unvalidated retention or Premium conversion.
