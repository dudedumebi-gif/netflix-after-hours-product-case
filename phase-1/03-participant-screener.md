# Participant Screener

## Purpose

Recruit adults with recent, relevant late-night streaming behaviour while preserving variation in device, household, schedule, and wearable use. The screener supports the survey, interviews, and usability sessions.

## Participant-facing introduction

We are conducting independent portfolio research about late-night video-streaming habits and product ideas. This research is not affiliated with Netflix or another streaming provider. Responses will be used only for product research. Do not share account credentials, medical records, or confidential information.

- Less than once per week - terminate

### S3. Late viewing

- 4-6 days per week
- 2-3 days per week
- About once per week
- Less than once per week - normally terminate

### S4. Relevant behaviour

During the past three months, which have happened while you were streaming video? Select all that apply.

- I fell asleep while content was still playing
- I became drowsy and stopped watching before falling asleep
- I intentionally used streaming video to help me wind down or fall asleep

- I remained awake while another person stopped paying attention
- None of these - terminate

- 8-30 days ago
- 1-3 months ago

### S12. Research participation

## Recruitment quotas

For 12-15 interviews:

- 4-5 habitual bedtime viewers
- 4-5 late-night series viewers with continuity problems
- 4-5 wearable-owning or audio-first viewers
- At least four shared-screen participants across the total sample
- At least two shift workers or travellers across the total sample
- No device category should represent more than 60% of the sample

For usability rounds, recruit at least two TV-first participants, two mobile-first participants, and one shared-screen participant per round where feasible.

## Exclusion and quality controls

- Exclude anyone under 18.
- Exclude professional market researchers working on streaming products if their expertise could dominate ordinary-user behaviour.
- Exclude participants who cannot describe a relevant experience within three months.
- Do not exclude people because they dislike the concept.
- Use one open-ended verification question before scheduling: “Briefly describe the last time you became drowsy or fell asleep while streaming.”
- Remove duplicate, nonsensical, copied, or speed-completed survey responses before analysis.
