# Hypothesis and Evidence Register

## Register rules

- **Status U:** Unvalidated hypothesis
- **Status P:** Partially supported by secondary evidence
- **Status V:** Validated by completed research meeting the evidence standard
- **Status R:** Rejected or materially contradicted
- **Risk:** Critical, High, Medium, or Low based on decision impact and uncertainty

All entries begin as unvalidated. Existing portfolio documents provide concept provenance, not customer validation.
