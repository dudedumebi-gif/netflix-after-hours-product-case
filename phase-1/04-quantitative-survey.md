# Quantitative Survey Instrument

## Administration

- Target 300 qualified responses; acceptable range 200-400.
- Estimated completion time: 8-10 minutes.
- Randomize feature statements and response options where marked.
- Keep screening outcomes separate from the qualified analysis denominator.
- Do not name the concept until after behaviour and problem questions.

## Introduction and consent

This independent portfolio research explores late-night video-streaming habits. It is not affiliated with Netflix or another streaming provider. Participation is voluntary. Responses will be analyzed in aggregate. Do not provide account credentials, medical records, or confidential information.

**Q1. Are you at least 18 and willing to participate under these conditions?**

- Yes
- No - terminate

## Feature-bundle test

Randomly assign respondents to review two of the following bundles:

- **Bundle A:** Manual timer and playback stop
- **Bundle B:** Timer, dimming, checkpoint, and recovery
- **Bundle C:** Bundle B plus wearable automation

For each assigned bundle:

**Q20. How likely would you be to enable this bundle?** Five-point likelihood scale.

**Q21. Would this change how positively you view the streaming service?** Five-point scale from much less positive to much more positive.

**Q22. Would this influence your decision to remain subscribed?**

- Small influence
- Moderate influence
- Strong influence
- Unsure

## Data-quality fields

- Record completion time.
- Include one instructed-response attention check outside a sensitive question.
- Flag duplicate response IDs or matching metadata where legally and ethically permitted.
- Predefine exclusion rules before analysis.
